Cosmos and progression

Understand levels, XP, streaks, and the review-topic map.

Cosmos is a read-only local progress view. It uses saved exam history and current Study Plan priorities; it does not use AI or create new records.

XP and levels

XP is calculated from saved exam results using Hamartia's exam progression formula. A level is derived from cumulative XP; the first threshold is 500 XP and later levels use the same 500-XP step. This is app progression, not a grade or learning score.

Study streak

The displayed streak is derived from dates on saved exam records. It counts a consecutive run ending today or yesterday; it is not a record of every study activity and does not mean learning occurred on each counted day. Missing or incorrectly dated exam entries affect it.

Review orbits

The map shows up to nine subject/topic groups with questions currently due for review. Groups with more pending missed-question records appear first. The topic labels and counts come from saved records and the deterministic Study Plan summary. Adding or reviewing a question changes the underlying record and therefore can change the map.

What Cosmos cannot tell you

XP, level, streak, and orbit position do not establish knowledge, mastery, effort, or future exam performance. A low or interrupted streak is not a judgment about the student. Use these displays as navigation cues only; check understanding with fresh practice and explanations. See Learning evidence and claims and Study workflows.
