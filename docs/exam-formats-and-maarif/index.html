Exam formats and Maarif

Current scoring support, curriculum references, and adaptation boundaries.

Hamartia grew from Turkish mock-exam workflows. Exam-format support is specific; free-text labels do not change scoring rules.

Current built-in exam support

The app records TYT/AYT full mock sessions and single-subject exams. Full mocks retain subject sections. Its net calculation uses the YKS wrong-answer penalty. Other systems with compatible scoring may reuse the workflow; formats such as LGS or different scoring rules need adaptation through a contribution or fork. The app does not create official exams, questions, or answer keys.

Curriculum is not an exam mode

Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli is a curriculum framework and assessment approach, not a national-exam name. Hamartia does not import MEB curriculum text or make official/verified alignment. A student may enter a reference on a missed question (framework, source/version, grade/year, unit/theme, paraphrased outcome, process component, optional URL). The URL is not fetched or verified. Study Agent context may include these user-entered references after consent.

Adapting a format

When contributing another exam system, document its session structure, section totals, scoring formula, validation limits, tests, and migration/backup compatibility. Keep labels and curriculum references separate from score calculation. See Contributing and Learning evidence.
