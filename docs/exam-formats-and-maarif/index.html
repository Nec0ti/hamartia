Exam formats and Maarif

Current scoring support, curriculum references, and adaptation boundaries.

Hamartia grew from Turkish mock-exam workflows. Exam-format support is specific; free-text labels do not change scoring rules.

Current exam and practice scoring

The app records TYT/AYT full mock sessions and single-subject exams; full mocks retain subject sections. Exams and completed practice sets share one append-only catalog of versioned scoring profiles, and each record stores the complete selected rule snapshot. Old records retain the built-in legacy-yks@1 rule: one point per correct answer, a 1/4-point penalty per incorrect answer, and zero for blanks. You can create custom profiles with a display name, country label, and exact rational points for correct, incorrect, and blank answers; updating a profile creates a new version and saved records keep their original rule. These labels and rules are user configuration, not official exam or national rules and not verified alignment with an authority or Maarif. Exam XP keeps its fixed raw-count activity formula; practice sets do not grant XP. Neither score nor XP is evidence of mastery. The app does not create official exams, questions, or answer keys.

Curriculum is not an exam mode

Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli is a curriculum framework and assessment approach, not a national-exam name. Hamartia does not import MEB curriculum text or make official/verified alignment. A student may enter a reference on a missed question (framework, source/version, grade/year, unit/theme, paraphrased outcome, process component, optional URL). The URL is not fetched or verified. Study Agent context may include these user-entered references after consent.

Adapting a format

When contributing another exam system, document its session structure, section totals, scoring formula, validation limits, tests, and migration/backup compatibility. Keep labels and curriculum references separate from score calculation. See Contributing and Learning evidence.

Official context: MEB Maarif assessment overview · Maarif common text (PDF) · 2026 YKS schedule · 2026 LGS guide (PDF). Check current official pages for later dates.
