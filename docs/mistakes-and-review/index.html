Mistakes and review

How saved mistakes, deterministic patterns, and due dates work.

A missed-question record is a prompt for later study, not a complete account of an exam or a verified diagnosis.

What a record contains

A record may contain text, an image, subject/topic, a built-in tag, linked exam details, and optional user-entered curriculum reference fields. Add only context you want to retain. To remove an image or change its labels, edit the question record.

Patterns shown by Study Plan

Study Plan counts saved pending questions by subject, topic, and available tags: Knowledge gap, Distractor trap, Misread the question, and Time pressure. If question number/order and time were recorded, it groups saved wrong questions into early, middle, and late portions and reports counts and median recorded time. These summaries cover saved mistakes only; they cannot infer the correct-answer denominator, so they are not an exam accuracy rate or a psychological diagnosis.

Due-date rules

New questions are due immediately. A wrong self-recorded review is due after one day. Consecutive correct reviews use intervals of 1, 3, 7, 14, then 30 days. Due dates use the device's local calendar day; older attempts without a saved local day use the legacy UTC fallback. Marking a question reviewed removes it from the active queue; it does not declare mastery.

Change a review mark

Use the undo action on a reviewed question to return it to the pending workflow. Edit or delete its saved record if its text, image, or labels are wrong. See Study workflows.
