Purpose and scope

What Hamartia stores, what it supports, and what it does not claim.

Hamartia is a local-first study record and review tool. It connects exam results, student-saved mistakes, topic practice, and scheduled review. Its summaries describe saved records; they do not certify learning.

Included workflows

  • TYT/AYT full mock and single-subject exam records.
  • Text or image missed-question records, subject/topic labels, and optional user-entered curriculum references.
  • Deterministic due-question and topic summaries; completed topic-practice records.
  • Cosmos progression summaries and review-topic orbits derived from saved records.
  • A shared Clock timer, Pomodoro, countdown, stopwatch/laps, and local reminders.
  • Optional Study Agent providers in the packaged desktop app and optional separate question analysis.

Limits

Hamartia does not provide exam questions or answer keys, import an official curriculum, verify user-entered curriculum references, or infer mastery from a score, tag, AI suggestion, or one correct answer. Exam records use a selected, versioned scoring profile: the built-in legacy YKS profile uses the YKS wrong-answer penalty, and students can create custom exact-rational rules and labels. A custom label is not official recognition or verified alignment with an exam authority. The app is not a cloud sync service or a wake-from-sleep alarm.
