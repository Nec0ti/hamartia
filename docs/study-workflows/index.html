Study workflows

How exam entry, saved mistakes, topic practice, and review connect.

The app keeps the student's records connected without requiring an AI call. A typical loop starts with an exam result, continues with selected missed questions, then returns to those questions or a topic practice session.

Record an exam

Dashboard accepts TYT/AYT full mocks, user-defined custom mocks, and single-subject exams. Custom mocks use your own format name, section labels, and selected scoring profile; their 12-section ceiling is a product safeguard, not an exam standard. Full mocks keep section results together while preserving section summaries. You can edit or delete a saved exam.

Scoring profiles and saved results

Select a versioned scoring profile before entering correct, incorrect, blank, and duration values. The legacy YKS profile applies a 1/4 wrong-answer penalty; custom profiles use the exact rational rule shown in the editor. Saved exams keep a snapshot of that rule, so editing a profile creates a new version without changing old results. XP remains based on the fixed raw-count activity formula and is not a score or measure of mastery.

Capture mistakes

Open My Mistakes or use the incorrect-answer entry from an exam. Save text, attach a supported image, add subject/topic labels, and optionally record exam question number, solve order, or time spent. Curriculum reference fields are optional and user-entered. Saved records can be edited, searched, reviewed, and deleted.

Study and review

Study Plan groups due questions by topic, shows available mistake tags and exam-order counts, and offers focused practice.

Practice records and trends

Each completed practice set stores the selected versioned scoring-profile snapshot alongside date, subject/topic, counts, and time. Create and edit the shared profile catalog from Dashboard → Scoring Profiles. The catalog accepts user-defined exact-rational rules; old sets keep the legacy YKS snapshot. Trends are separated by subject, topic, profile, and version. Accuracy uses correct + incorrect, so blanks are excluded; practice sets do not grant XP.

Read progress carefully

Exam, review, practice, and Clock histories report what was entered. Missing records, free-text labels, and self-reported answers affect their summaries. Cosmos adds XP, level, streak, and review-topic orbits from saved records; those are progression displays, not evidence of mastery. See Cosmos and progression, Mistakes and review, and Learning evidence.
