Study workflows

How exam entry, saved mistakes, topic practice, and review connect.

The app keeps the student's records connected without requiring an AI call. A typical loop starts with an exam result, continues with selected missed questions, then returns to those questions or a topic practice session.

Record an exam

Dashboard accepts TYT/AYT full mocks and single-subject exams. Full mocks keep section results together while preserving subject summaries. Enter correct, incorrect, blank, and duration values. The net preview uses the built-in YKS penalty formula. You can edit or delete a saved exam.

Capture mistakes

Open My Mistakes or use the incorrect-answer entry from an exam. Save text, attach a supported image, add subject/topic labels, and optionally record exam question number, solve order, or time spent. Curriculum reference fields are optional and user-entered. Saved records can be edited, searched, reviewed, and deleted.

Study and review

Study Plan groups due questions by topic, shows available mistake tags and exam-order counts, and offers focused practice. A completed practice set records date, subject/topic, correct/incorrect/blank counts, and time. The trend summary is computed from completed sets; blanks are excluded from the answered-accuracy denominator.

Read progress carefully

Exam, review, practice, and Clock histories report what was entered. Missing records, free-text labels, and self-reported answers affect their summaries. See Mistakes and review and Learning evidence.
