Hamartia social User Agreement

Version label: v0.1-draft · Status: not approved and not effective.

DRAFT — NOT APPROVED. This page is a review placeholder, not a contract or legal advice. Do not accept it as final terms. Social sign-in remains disabled until qualified review approves a complete version.

This draft does not establish final terms. It intentionally leaves material terms unresolved instead of inventing them. The social account and profile feature is a prototype; public launch and real-user onboarding are gated.

Items requiring review and completion

  • Legal identity and contact details of the service operator.
  • Eligibility, age and guardian-consent requirements for students and adults.
  • Account rules, acceptable use, suspension, termination, and appeals.
  • Which social profile fields are processed, their purposes, and any future visibility behavior.
  • Data retention, deletion, account closure, backups, and incident handling.
  • Service availability, limitations, governing terms, and dispute process.
  • Review against the final privacy notice and the actual hosting/data architecture.

Do not rely on this placeholder for rights, obligations, consent, or legal protection. The linked Privacy and consent guide describes prototype behavior and is also subject to review before launch.
